4. Engineering Notes / Model Limitations
Checks explicitly included in this revision
• Separate service bearing and ultimate structural demand checks. • Biaxial service eccentricity with no-tension contact requirement. • Two-axis overturning stability. • One-way shear checks in both footing directions. • Punching shear using the d/2 critical perimeter and enclosed critical area. • Flexural demand in both directions with factored moments treated conservatively. • Minimum reinforcement check against the adopted 0.0018 ratio used by this tool. • Input validation, zero-load handling, unit conversion without resetting user inputs, and visible calculation errors.
